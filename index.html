Tailored learning for every milestone

Our classrooms are divided into program zones that match each stage of early childhood, from first friends to primary-school readiness.

  1. 🧸 Playgroup

    Age 2 to 3 · The Zone of Discovery

    Sensory exploration with Montessori materials. Music, finger painting, block building and storytelling build language, fine motor skills and the joy of friends.

  2. 🌈 Nursery

    Age 3 to 4 · The Zone of Expression

    Vocabulary and imagination take off. Pre-reading, pre-writing and basic maths come through games and theme-based Play-way projects.

  3. 🔤 Junior KG

    Age 4 to 5 · The Zone of Logic

    Phonics, sight words and counting with visual aids and logic puzzles. Children form full sentences and share their own thoughts clearly.

  4. 🎓 Senior KG

    Age 5 to 6 · The Zone of Leadership

    Reading readiness, maths problem-solving, environmental awareness and public speaking for a smooth move to primary school.
